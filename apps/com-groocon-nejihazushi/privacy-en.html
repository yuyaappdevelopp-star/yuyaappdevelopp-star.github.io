LITTLE WORKSHOP

Your privacy

Your little workshop stays on your device. The app does not send your play records out.

Offline · No account · No ads

Information stored on your device

The app stores puzzle progress, completed levels, personal best temporary-slot usage, and music on/off and volume, sound effects, and haptics preferences in its local storage. These records let you resume puzzles, display your personal bests, and keep your settings.

No transmission by the app

The app has no process that sends these records to the developer or third parties. It includes no ads, analytics, tracking, or account registration.

Device permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, photos, contacts, location, or microphone. The game works without an internet connection.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain on the device. Deleting the app removes its local records. iOS backups and restoration follow Apple’s system behavior.

Support requests

Information that you choose to send in a support email is separate from automatic collection by the app. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information in your request.

About this website

These help pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This is separate from the app, which does not transmit your play records.
