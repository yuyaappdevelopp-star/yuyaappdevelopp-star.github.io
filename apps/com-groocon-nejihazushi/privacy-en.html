Privacy in your workshop

Your puzzle saves stay on your device. Here is how the advertising version handles data.

Which versions this covers

This page explains version 1.3 and later, which add advertising, and earlier versions. Version 1.2 and earlier contain no advertising SDK and do not automatically send app data to the developer or an ad service.

What stays on your device

Puzzle progress, completed tools, best temporary-slot usage, and music, sound, and haptic settings are saved locally. Version 1.3 also stores ad timing, the ten-minute ad-free break, and verified early-player access. The app does not send your puzzle saves to an ad company.

Ads and consent in version 1.3

The free version uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. Ads load after the applicable regional consent and privacy choices are checked. When Ad privacy choices appears in Settings, you can revisit your choices there. All 18 puzzles, hints, and Undo remain available without watching ads.

Information handled by advertising SDKs

Google advertising and consent SDKs may handle IP addresses and approximate region, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash information, performance data, and other diagnostics. Uses include ad delivery and measurement, fraud prevention, analytics, consent management, and SDK improvement. The app does not request location permission or collect precise location.

Ad personalization

The app requests non-personalized ads and disables Google publisher first-party ID. It does not request Apple tracking permission or authorize access to IDFA. See the Google links below for further information.

Early players

Players whose signed App Store transaction verifies an original iOS build of 1, 2, or 3 keep ad-free access. Ads also stay disabled when the purchase cannot be verified. This check uses Apple StoreKit; the app does not send transaction information to a developer server.

Permissions and offline play

The app does not request camera, photos, contacts, location, or microphone access. Puzzles and saves work offline. Ads, purchase verification, and support pages may require an internet connection.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app removes its local records. An iOS backup may restore them. Information handled by Google is retained and can be managed under Google privacy policies.

Contact and this website

Information you choose to send by email, including your reply address, is used to respond to your inquiry. This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub records access IP addresses for security and related purposes; this is separate from your local puzzle saves.
