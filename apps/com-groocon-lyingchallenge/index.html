Can you tell when they’re lying?

Lying Challenge is a two-player real-world bluffing game for iPhone.

Get a bluff idea from the app or make up your own. Hide an object, make your claim, test each other with questions and actions, then reveal the truth.

2 players. 1 phone. All bluff.

No account required. No camera or microphone required.
