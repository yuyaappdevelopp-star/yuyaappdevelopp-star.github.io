Privacy Policy

App: Slow Down Video

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

Slow Down Video (“the App”) is an iOS application provided by Yuya Takamoto (“the Developer”).

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

■ Video Data

The App allows you to select videos from your photo library for purposes such as changing playback speed, previewing the result, and exporting an edited video.

Video editing and export processing are performed on your device.

Videos selected by the user are not uploaded to servers operated by the Developer for video processing.

Edited videos are saved to the user's photo library only when the user chooses to save them.

■ Advertising

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

For purposes such as serving ads, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving services, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information including:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising and ad interaction data
  • App usage and product interaction data
  • Crash information
  • Performance and diagnostic information

The information actually processed may vary depending on the user's region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's advertising configuration.

The App does not provide the contents of videos selected by the user to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

For more information about Google's data practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

■ Advertising Privacy Choices

The App uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to provide advertising consent and privacy choices required in certain regions.

When available, users may review or change advertising privacy choices through the “Privacy Choices” option in the App.

The App does not independently display an AppTrackingTransparency permission prompt and does not directly request access to the IDFA.

■ In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase that permanently removes advertisements.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

The Developer does not directly receive or store credit card numbers, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Apple StoreKit purchase information is used to determine whether the Remove Ads purchase is active.

■ Accounts

No account registration or login is required to use the App.

The Developer does not require profile information such as your name, home address, or phone number to use the App.

■ Support Communications

If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer may receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you voluntarily provide.

This information is used only as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, investigate issues, and provide support.

Support communications may be retained for a reasonable period as necessary to provide support and maintain appropriate records, and may subsequently be deleted.

■ Data Retention and Deletion

Videos selected for editing are not stored on servers operated by the Developer.

Advertising-related information is processed and retained according to Google's services and privacy policies.
